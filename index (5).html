Letters to Investors

Updates from Fourth Mark.

Dear Investor,

Today the fund bought $163 of Intel (INTC) put options expiring December 4, 2026.

Three developments shaped this decision.

First, leadership at Nvidia. On November 23, Nvidia’s board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO, less than two weeks after his resignation. The market rallied on the news, but in my view his return does not answer the larger question facing the sector: whether the scale of AI infrastructure spending can earn an adequate return. An industry this large should not rest on the confidence placed in one person.

Second, politics. Democrats won control of both the House and the Senate in this month’s midterm elections, and several campaigned against the growth of AI data centers and their effect on electricity costs. I expect the new Congress to put AI spending under closer scrutiny.

Third, trade. The Wall Street Journal reported today that the administration is preparing to raise tariffs on China, the European Union and 14 other economies, with rates for partners such as the EU, Japan and South Korea rising as high as the 15% ceilings in their trade agreements. The final rates and timing are not yet settled, but in my view higher tariffs would add pressure on chipmakers.

Taken together, I do not believe the recent market decline is over, and this position reflects that view.

Thank you,
Thomas
